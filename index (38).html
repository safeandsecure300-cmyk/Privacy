1

Introduction

Ghosh Bari Park Bot is a Telegram bot created to help people explore and stay informed about Ghosh Bari Park at Gulai Chandi, Basirhat, North 24 Parganas, West Bengal, India. Depending on which features are switched on, it may answer commands, share park information and updates, and let visitors send posts, feedback, reports and suggestions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot and this website may process, why, who may see it, how long it may be kept, and what choices you have.

Independent project. The bot is developed and run independently by Sahin. It is not an officially authorised service of any park owner, park management or government body unless written authorisation is obtained and stated in this policy.

2

Information We May Process

Depending on the features that are enabled and the code that is deployed, the bot may process the following categories of information:

  • Telegram identifiers: your Telegram user ID, first name and username, which Telegram provides to a bot when you interact with it.
  • Commands and messages: the commands you press or type and the messages you send to the bot.
  • Feedback and suggestions: text you choose to submit about the park or the bot.
  • Reports and support requests: problems, complaints or questions you send to the developer or administrators.
  • Media and files: photos, videos, captions and other files you upload.
  • Profile and post history: information in a profile, if profiles are enabled, and a record of posts you submit.
  • Administrative and moderation records: approvals, rejections, warnings, blocks and notes made by administrators or managers.
  • Technical records: logs, timestamps and error records created while the bot runs.

Telegram does not give bots your phone number by default.

This list describes categories that may be processed. It is not a statement that every category is collected today. Actual processing depends on the enabled features and the deployed code.

3

Purposes of Processing

Information may be used to:

  • respond to your commands and button presses;
  • provide park-related information and updates;
  • receive and manage visitor feedback;
  • handle reports and suggestions;
  • review, manage and, where you ask for it, publish submitted posts;
  • maintain profiles, if that feature is enabled;
  • moderate content and prevent spam or abuse;
  • keep the service secure and fix technical problems;
  • meet legal obligations.

The bot is not intended to use your information for advertising.

4

Legal Basis and User Choices

Processing must comply with the law that applies to it, which may include India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules as they come into force. Depending on the situation, processing may rest on:

  • Your consent, for example when you choose to submit a post, photo or feedback;
  • Legitimate operational purposes, such as keeping the bot working, preventing spam and protecting its security;
  • Legal obligations, where the law requires information to be kept or disclosed.

No single basis applies to everything. The basis depends on what the information is and why it is processed.

Your choices

  • You can use the bot's basic commands without submitting optional information.
  • You can decide not to send feedback, posts, photos, videos or profile details.
  • You can stop using the bot, or block it, at any time in Telegram.
  • You can ask about access, correction or deletion as described in Section 12.

Storage Architecture

A Telegram bot of this kind can keep different kinds of data in different places. The separation described here is a possible design, not a description of what is running today.

Application hosting
The server that runs the bot's code and talks to Telegram.
Database records
Structured records such as user IDs, profile fields, post status, moderation notes and settings.
Uploaded media
Photos, videos and files submitted by users, which may be kept in cloud object storage.
Private storage
Content that is not meant to be public, such as pending submissions, reports and administrative records.
Publicly published content
Approved posts or media that are shown in a Telegram channel or similar public place.
Backups and logs
Copies of data kept for recovery, and technical logs kept for troubleshooting and security.

Only the components the developer has actually configured should be treated as operational.

7

Data Security

The developer aims to apply reasonable security practices, including:

  • limiting administrative access to people who need it;
  • protecting the bot token, API keys and other credentials, and not publishing them;
  • using private storage for content that should not be public;
  • keeping software and dependencies up to date;
  • applying access controls to servers, storage and admin tools;
  • monitoring errors and unusual activity;
  • testing recovery from backups, where backups exist.

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee that information will never be lost, accessed or disclosed without authorisation.

Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be shared or disclosed:

  • With Telegram, because the bot works through the Telegram platform;
  • With infrastructure providers that are actually used to host or store data;
  • When you ask for publication, for example when you submit a post that is then published;
  • When legally required, such as in response to a valid legal request;
  • To investigate abuse or protect the security of the bot, its users and its administrators.

Personal information is not intended to be sold.

External services are involved: Telegram always, and hosting or storage providers once they are in use. This policy does not claim that no third parties process data.

9

Public Posts and Media

Posts, photos, videos and captions that are approved and published may become visible to anyone who can see the channel or place where they appear. Once something is public, others may copy, save or share it.

Before you submit: do not send confidential information, and do not send another person's private content, photo or video unless you have appropriate permission.

Deleting your original Telegram message may not delete a separate copy that the bot has stored. To ask for removal of stored or published content, contact the developer as described in Section 12.

10

Cookies and Tracking

This website is a single static page. It does not set cookies, and it contains no analytics, advertising trackers, social media widgets, third-party scripts, remote fonts or remote images.

Theme preference

If you press the theme button, the page saves your light or dark choice in your browser's local storage so it can be applied next time. This stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be removed by clearing site data in your browser. If storage is blocked, the page still works and simply does not remember your choice.

What this does not cover

The service that delivers this page to you may keep ordinary technical logs, such as IP address, time and browser type, under its own policies. The developer does not add any tracking to this page. Links to Telegram and other sites leave this website and are covered by those sites' policies (see Section 15).

Data Retention

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in Section 3, and subject to legal and operational requirements.

  • Records and media may be kept while a feature, post or profile is active, or while needed to handle a report or dispute.
  • Logs may be kept for a limited time for troubleshooting and security.
  • Backups, where they exist, may keep older copies until they are replaced or removed, so deleted data may remain in a backup for a while.

No fixed retention periods are stated here because none have been configured. If specific periods are set, they will be added to this section.

Data Access and Deletion Requests

Where the law gives you the right, you may ask to access, correct or delete information the bot holds about you.

How to ask

Send a message to the developer on Telegram: @Sahin_09022. Say what you are asking for, and include the Telegram username or ID you used with the bot, and, for a removal request, what content you mean.

What to expect

  • We may need to verify that you are the person the information relates to before acting.
  • Some records may have to be kept where the law requires it, or where needed to deal with abuse or a dispute.
  • Content already shared in a public channel may need to be removed there as well as from the bot's own storage.
